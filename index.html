Common questions

What does Legacy buy?

Gold, silver, firearms, electronics and collectibles. Guns and gold are what we buy most, and we also buy jewelry, sports memorabilia, musical instruments, art and tools. If you are not sure, call (480) 876-5770 or bring it in.

Do you sell, too?

Yes. Legacy is a buy, sell and trade shop. Stop in to see what is in the case, or trade something you have toward something you want.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome Mon–Fri 9–5 and Sat 11–5. For a large collection or an estate, call ahead so we can set aside time.

What do I need to bring?

The item and a valid government-issued photo ID.

Do I have to sell?

No. We look it over at the counter and make you an offer. You decide: sell it, trade it, or take it home.
