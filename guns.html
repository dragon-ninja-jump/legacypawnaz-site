Common questions

Do you buy inherited guns?

Yes. Inherited guns and estate collections are a big part of what we buy. Bring them in, or call (480) 876-5770 first if it is a large collection.

What do I need to bring?

The firearm and a valid government-issued photo ID. You do not need the original box or receipt.

How much will you pay for my gun?

It depends on the make, model, condition and current demand. Bring it in and we will make you an offer in person. There is no obligation to sell.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during business hours. For a large collection or an estate, call ahead so we can set aside time.

Do you buy ammunition and parts?

Yes. We buy ammunition, magazines, optics, parts and accessories.
